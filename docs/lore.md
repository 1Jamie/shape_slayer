## Foundation

### Core Concept
Cards are **memories** - fragments of a forgotten past that players recover and repair through mastery progression. As players progress, they remember more of their past, their purpose, and the truth about the world.

### The Four Troublemakers
Four shapes remember more than the others. Each has a distinct voice and role:

- **Blue (Square/Warrior)**: The historian. Remembers the kingdom, the empire, the Sorcerer's arrival. Holds the most complete memories of the past.
- **Pink (Triangle/Rogue)**: The innocent. Asks questions, doesn't remember much. Represents curiosity and the desire to understand.
- **Red (Pentagon/Tank)**: The rebel. Remembers being a troublemaker. Represents defiance and resistance to order.
- **Purple (Hexagon/Mage)**: The conflicted. Defensive about magic users, but also proud. Represents the complexity of identity.

## The World

### The Kingdom Before
- A kingdom/empire existed before the Sorcerer's arrival
- It was a place of magic, diversity, and life
- The four troublemakers were important figures in the kingdom
- The kingdom had its own culture, people, and history

### The Sorcerer's Arrival
- A Sorcerer claiming to be an "apostle of the god of order" arrived
- At first, he seemed helpful - solving problems, stopping conflicts
- He claimed to be bringing order to the world
- But his true goal was to remove everything that caused "disorder"

### The Order's Victory
- The Sorcerer removed everything he believed caused disorder
- This included memories, identities, and the kingdom itself
- All that remains are basic geometric shapes
- Most shapes are mindless, only attacking when they see the players
- The four players are "troublemakers" - those who refused to fully accept the Order

### The Geometric World
- The world after the Order's victory
- Everything is reduced to geometric shapes
- The world is fragmented, rooms shift and change
- Memories exist as fragments scattered throughout (cards)
- The world is hostile, but also holds the key to recovery

## The Time Loop and The Nexus

### The Time Loop (The Echo)
- **Origin**: Unknown (bootstrap paradox - the echo exists because it was created, but who created it is lost to time)
- **Possible Creators**: The kingdom's last mages, a future version of the four, an unknown ally, or the kingdom itself
- **Nature**: An echo - a snapshot and forever memory of what was, preserved in time
- **Purpose**: To preserve the kingdom's memory forever, frozen in its final moments
- **Mechanism**: The echo resets the four's personal memories each cycle, but the Nexus remembers everything they recover

### The Nexus
- **What It Is**: A fragment of the old kingdom, preserved in the void between the loop and the geometric world
- **Why It's Special**: It's the anchor point of the time loop - it exists outside the loop's reset
- **Why They Return**: The Nexus pulls them back when they "die" - they're bound to it as the loop's preserved ones
- **Why They Don't Die**: The loop preserves their existence - they can't truly die while the loop exists
- **The Void**: The space between the loop and the geometric world - infinite potential, but starts empty

### The Loop's True Nature
- The loop is an **echo** - a snapshot and forever memory of what was
- It preserves the kingdom's final moments, frozen in time
- Each cycle through the echo brings back fragments:
  - **Memories** (cards) - Pieces of the kingdom's history, recovered from the echo
  - **Shards** - Crystallized essence, remnants of the old world
  - **People** (NPCs) - Echoes of the old kingdom, rescued from the geometric world
  - **Materials** - Fragments of the past, used to build the Nexus
  - **Knowledge** - Understanding unlocked through exploration of the echo
- The Nexus grows with each cycle, built from the fragments recovered from the echo

## The Endings

### Ending 1: Breaking the Loop (The "Wrong" Choice)
- **How to Achieve**: After defeating the final boss, choose "Break the Loop" (requires finding a specific item/memory)
- **What Happens**: 
  - The Nexus collapses (it was anchored to the loop)
  - The geometric world returns to normal (the Order wins completely)
  - They're free, but everything they built is gone
  - The kingdom's memory is lost forever
  - They're alone in a world that's already been consumed
- **Tone**: Somber, tragic - they realize they made a mistake
- **Achievement**: "The Price of Freedom" - Break the loop and lose everything

### Ending 2: Staying in the Loop (The "True" Ending)
- **How to Achieve**: After defeating the final boss, choose "Continue the Loop" (requires building the Nexus to a certain level)
- **What Happens**:
  - They accept their role as eternal guardians
  - The Nexus continues to grow
  - They build a new kingdom in the void
  - The old kingdom's memory is preserved forever
  - They find peace and purpose
  - The echo becomes a gift, not a curse
  - **Final Milestone**: Restore the Queen, their close friend and the kingdom's last ruler
- **Tone**: Hopeful, beautiful - they find meaning in their eternal role
- **Achievement**: "Eternal Kingdom" - Build the Nexus into a kingdom and accept your role
- **Ultimate Achievement**: "The Queen's Return" - Restore the Queen and complete the kingdom's restoration

## The Nexus Kingdom

### Expansion System
- **NPCs**: Rescued from the geometric world, appear as the Nexus grows
- **Buildings**: Constructed from resources gathered during runs
- **Zones**: Different areas of the Nexus that unlock as it expands
- **Customization**: Players can place buildings, decorations, and create their own layout

### Resource Acquisition
- **Memories (Cards)**: Recovered from the geometric world, stored in the Library
- **Shards**: Crystallized essence from defeated enemies, used to build structures
- **Materials**: Stone, crystal, memory fragments, essence from runs
- **People (NPCs)**: Rescued from the geometric world, appear after certain conditions
- **Knowledge**: Unlocks new buildings, features, gained from achievements

### Building the Kingdom
- Start with an empty void and just the portal
- Each cycle, the Nexus grows - new NPCs, buildings, areas
- Eventually becomes a full kingdom in the void
- The echo becomes the source for recovery and building, not just survival

### The Queen's Restoration
- The ultimate goal: Restore the Queen, the kingdom's last ruler
- The Queen was an extremely close and personal friend of the four troublemakers
- Restoring the Queen requires:
  - Recovering all major memories (specific cards unlocked)
  - Building the Nexus to a certain level (all major buildings, 50+ NPCs)
  - Completing specific achievements
  - Reaching the true ending (choosing to stay in the loop)
- The Queen's restoration is the final success of rebuilding the kingdom
- Once restored, the Queen helps guide the Nexus's growth and provides closure to the story

## Narrative Themes

### Memory and Identity
- Who were they? Who are they now?
- The struggle to recover lost memories
- Finding identity through recovered fragments

### Order vs. Freedom
- The Sorcerer's Order vs. their resistance
- The tension between structure and chaos
- Finding meaning in resistance

### Loss and Recovery
- What was lost, what can be regained
- The kingdom's memory preserved through the loop
- Building something new from the old

### Unity and Diversity
- Four different perspectives, one goal
- The power of working together
- Each character's unique role

### Power and Responsibility
- How to use their restored memories/powers
- The responsibility of preserving the kingdom's memory
- The choice to continue or break the loop

### Acceptance and Purpose
- Finding meaning in the eternal loop
- Accepting their role as guardians
- Building a new kingdom from the old

## Key Questions (Left Ambiguous)

### The Mystery
- **Who cast the loop?** - Unknown (bootstrap paradox, kingdom, future selves, or other)
- **When was it cast?** - Before or during the Order's final assault
- **Why were they chosen?** - Were they chosen, or were they just there?
- **Can the loop be broken?** - Technically yes, but it's the wrong choice
- **What is the loop's ultimate purpose?** - To preserve the kingdom's memory and allow them to build a new one

### The Hope
- The Nexus grows with each cycle
- They can't escape, but they can evolve
- Each cycle, they add to the Nexus, making it more complete
- Eventually, the Nexus becomes a new kingdom - a place worth being in
- They continue because they choose to, not because they have to

## Implementation Notes

### Gameplay Integration
- The loop explains why players can keep playing after "beating" the game
- The Nexus expansion provides meaningful meta-progression
- Resource acquisition gives purpose to repeated runs
- Customization makes the Nexus feel personal and unique

### Narrative Integration
- Cards as memories ties progression to story
- Dialog reveals the truth gradually
- The twist ending recontextualizes everything
- The true ending provides closure and meaning

### Player Agency
- Players can choose to break the loop (wrong choice) or stay (true ending)
- Customization allows personal expression
- Building the Nexus is a collaborative effort across cycles
- The choice to continue becomes meaningful, not forced
